*** MUST HAVE PCA OR HHA CERTIFICATE TO APPLY ***

Galaxy Home Care ,  the fastest growing regional home care agency, is seeking certified Home Health Aides and Personal Care Aides for its Brooklyn, NY location.

FREE PRE-EMPLOYMENT PHYSICAL UPON HIRE!

Ready to make a difference in someone's life while taking your career to the next level?

  • Whether you prefer DAY, EVENING or NIGHT shifts , LIVE-IN or LIVE-OUT opportunities, Galaxy Home Care has the cases you are looking for!
  • Galaxy Home Care is one of the largest employers of certified home health aides in the region. At Galaxy Home Care we make our employees a top priority.
  • Galaxy Home Care will provide you with the same level of care and dedication that you provide your patients.
  • Our Patient Coordinators will work hard to accommodate your career preferences. As a member of our dynamic healthcare team, you will choose from prestigious assignments.
  • Our supportive staff will match your skills, experience, and schedule to find you the best long-term, short- term or travel assignments.

You must:

  • Care for clients in a professional and caring manner
  • Have reliable transportation
  • Successfully completed HHA and/or PCA training

Must Have:

  • Valid Home Health Aide (HHA) or Personal Care Aide (PCA) Certificate
  • Current Physical
  • Current PPD or proof of a chest x-ray if positive PPD
  • Current Drug screen and lab work
  • Rubella and Rubeola Immunizations and lab work
  • Influenza Vaccine
  • Two forms of identification
  • Most recent Covid-19 screening
